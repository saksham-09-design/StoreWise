## 🛍️ StoreWise – Smart Shop Management System

**StoreWise** is a comprehensive and user-friendly shop management system designed to help small and medium-sized businesses streamline their daily operations. From tracking purchases and managing inventory to handling sales and monitoring stock levels, StoreWise empowers shopkeepers to run their stores efficiently and make data-driven decisions.

### 🚀 Features

- 📦 Inventory & Stock Management  
- 💰 Purchase and Sales Tracking  
- 📊 Daily, Monthly, and Custom Reports  
- 🔔 Low Stock Alerts  
- 🧾 Invoice Generation  
- 🔍 Product Search & Filter  
- 📁 Data Storage with Backup Options
